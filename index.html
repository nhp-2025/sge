Why I built it

Three things a newsroom cannot see.

01

The source map leaves with the reporter

Who was reliable, who spoke under what conditions, who was never going to call back. It lives in one person's head and one person's notebook, and community newsrooms feel that hardest.

02

Who and what you typically rely on

Which people a beat keeps going back to, which institutions end up speaking for a community, which documents the reporting rests on. Questions about a body of work, not one story. Nobody answers them from memory.

03

What a story rests on, for the reader

Someone checking a claim goes to a search engine or a chatbot, not to the reporting the newsroom did. That is only solvable once the first two are.

Follow where this goes

Occasional notes as this moves toward a newsroom pilot: what the partner conversations settle, and when there is something to try. No more than a few a year.
